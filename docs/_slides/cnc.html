Introduction to Creation Nation Makerspace - Purpose of the training session - What you’ll learn today -horizontal- ## **Slide 2: What is CNC Routing?** - CNC (Computer Numerical Control) is a form of subtractive manufacturing. - CNC routers cut, carve, and engrave materials using computer-generated toolpaths. - Common applications: woodworking, sign-making, prototyping, and precision machining. -horizontoal- ### **CNC Router Components** - **Spindle** – Rotates the cutting tool at high speeds. - **Collet & End Mills** – Holds and rotates cutting tools. - **Worktable** – Surface where material is secured. - **X, Y, Z Axes** – Defines movement in 3D space. - **Dust Collection System** – Helps keep the workspace clean and safe. -vertical- ### Shapeoko 3 XXL -horizontal- ### CNC Workflow Overview 1. **Design (CAD Software)** – Create your project in a software like VCarve Pro. 2. **Toolpath Generation (CAM Software)** – Define how the machine will cut. 3. **Export G-code** – The machine-readable instructions. 4. **Machine Setup** – Secure material, install the correct tool, and zero the machine. 5. **Run the Job** – Monitor and adjust as needed. 6. **Cleanup & Shutdown** – Remove debris, store tools, and inspect your workpiece. -horizontal- ### CNC Safety Guidelines - Wear **safety glasses** and **hearing protection**. - Tie back long hair and avoid loose clothing. - Keep hands clear of the spindle and moving parts. - Never leave the CNC router unattended while cutting. - Know the location of the emergency stop button. -horizontal- ## Software - CAD/CAM - Intro to CAD Software (Design) - Overview of Popular CAD Software - Intro to CAM Software (Toolpaths) - Overview of Popular CAM Software -
