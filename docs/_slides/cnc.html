Introduction to Creation Nation Makerspace - Purpose of the training session - What you’ll learn today -horizontal- ## **Slide 2: What is CNC Routing?** - CNC (Computer Numerical Control) is a form of subtractive manufacturing. - CNC routers cut, carve, and engrave materials using computer-generated toolpaths. - Common applications: woodworking, sign-making, prototyping, and precision machining. -horizontoal- ## **CNC Router Components** - **Spindle** – Rotates the cutting tool at high speeds. - **Collet & End Mills** – Holds and rotates cutting tools. - **Worktable** – Surface where material is secured. - **X, Y, Z Axes** – Defines movement in 3D space. - **Dust Collection System** – Helps keep the workspace clean and safe. -vertical- ### Shapeoko 3 XXL -horizontal- ## **Slide 4: CNC Workflow Overview** 1. **Design (CAD Software)** – Create your project in a software like VCarve Pro. 2. **Toolpath Generation (CAM Software)** – Define how the machine will cut. 3. **Export G-code** – The machine-readable instructions. 4. **Machine Setup** – Secure material, install the correct tool, and zero the machine. 5. **Run the Job** – Monitor and adjust as needed. 6. **Cleanup & Shutdown** – Remove debris, store tools, and inspect your workpiece. -horizontal- ## **Slide 5: CNC Safety Guidelines** - Wear **safety glasses** and **hearing protection**. - Tie back long hair and avoid loose clothing. - Keep hands clear of the spindle and moving parts. - Never leave the CNC router unattended while cutting. - Know the location of the emergency stop button. -horizontal- ## Software - CAD/CAM - Intro to CAD Software (Design) - Overview of Popular CAD Software - Intro to CAM Software (Toolpaths) - Overview of Popular CAM Software - Converting CAD designs to CAM toolpaths -horizontal- ## **Slide 6: Introduction to VCarve Pro** - Creating a new project (setting dimensions and material type). - Importing vector files (DXF, SVG, AI, etc.). - Choosing the right tool for the job (end mill selection). - Setting up toolpaths (pocketing, profiling, engraving, etc.). --- ## **Slide 7: Running the CNC Router** 1. **Power on the CNC router** and check for readiness. 2. **Load your G-code file into gSender.** 3. **Secure the material** using clamps or vacuum hold-down. 4. **Zero the machine (X, Y, Z)** – Set your work origin. 5. **Run a Dry Test (Air Cut)** – Verify tool movement before cutting. 6. **Start the job** and monitor progress. 7. **Finish & Cleanup** – Power down, remove workpiece, clean the workspace. --- -horizontal- ## CNC Operation and workflow -
